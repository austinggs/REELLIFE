WORLD 02 — PLANETARY GEOGRAPHY

Defines the physical planet underlying ReelLife.

Core:
- Physical planet is the foundation.
- Hemispheres, latitude/longitude, elevation, terrain, water, climate, ecology, geology.
- Planet size follows travel/simulation/player scale rather than an Earth requirement.
- Coordinates, distance, direction, containment, adjacency, accessibility.
- Physical hierarchy distinct from human geography.
- Continents, oceans/seas, coastlines, mountains, rivers/lakes, terrain, climate, ecological regions, resources, geological regions, natural hazards.
- Hazard ≠ disaster.
- Accessibility, constraints, geographic history, persistence and spatial queries.
- Procedural generation may be rule-based and reproducible; authored and generated geography may coexist.
- Map is presentation over authoritative geography; player knowledge can be incomplete.
- Levels of spatial detail support performance.

Core principle: Geography is causal infrastructure.
