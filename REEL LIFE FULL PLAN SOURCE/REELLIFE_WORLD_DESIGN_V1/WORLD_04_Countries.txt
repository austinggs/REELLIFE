WORLD 04 — COUNTRIES

Defines countries as persistent territorial, institutional, economic, cultural, and historical environments.

Core:
- Country combines territory, population, institutions, laws, economy, culture, history, infrastructure, international relationships.
- CountryId, official/common/historical names, symbols, capital, founding date, constitutional history.
- Territory distinct from claims/control; government distinct from country.
- Citizenship distinct from residence, nationality, ethnicity, birthplace.
- Population dynamic and internally diverse; no single national personality.
- Currency and monetary architecture.
- Economy is derived, not a magical wealth score.
- Economic specialization emerges from geography, resources, labor, education, infrastructure, history, technology, institutions, trade, investment.
- Regional variation, urbanization, infrastructure, public services, resources, international relationships, country reputation, information/security/environmental conditions.
- Country creation, transformation, dissolution; authored major countries and procedural support for minor content.
- Country is a simulation context, not a giant NPC.

Core principle: A country is a persistent territorial, institutional, economic, cultural, and historical environment.
