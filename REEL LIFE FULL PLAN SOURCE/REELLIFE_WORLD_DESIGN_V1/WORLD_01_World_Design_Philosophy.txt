WORLD 01 — WORLD DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Defines the philosophy and governing principles for ReelLife's completely fictional, persistent world.

Core principles:
- Fictional, internally consistent, persistent, fully simulated world.
- Player is a participant, not the center.
- Fictional does not mean arbitrary.
- Physical geography → climate/environment → resources/ecology → settlement → population → infrastructure → institutions → economy → culture/society → organizations → households → individuals → decisions.
- Feedback loops, multiple causes, multiple consequences, uneven development, diversity without stereotypes.
- Institutions are real actors; the world is imperfectly known.
- Reality → information → perception → cognition → belief → decision.
- World does not bend around player; emergence is preferred over scripts.
- Uncertainty exists without randomness replacing causality.
- Layered world scale/resolution, validation, canon/state/history/belief separation, multiple modes, discoverability.

World Design Law: conditions → decisions → events → consequences → new conditions.

Core principle: The world should feel as though it existed before the player and continues after the player.
