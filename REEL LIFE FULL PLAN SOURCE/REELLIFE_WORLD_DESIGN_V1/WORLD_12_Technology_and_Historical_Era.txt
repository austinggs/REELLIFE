WORLD 12 — TECHNOLOGY & HISTORICAL ERA

Defines technological civilization development and historical technological context.

Core:
- Invention → development → production → adoption → diffusion → access → use → improvement → obsolescence.
- Technology is multidimensional, not a single score.
- Categories: energy, transportation, communication, computing, medicine, agriculture, manufacturing, construction, household, military.
- Technology definition includes TechnologyId, name, category, prerequisites, capabilities, inputs, outputs, production, knowledge, infrastructure, skills, cost, reliability, efficiency, risks, environmental effects, history, adoption, successors.
- Technology ≠ product; invention/research/scientific knowledge distinct.
- Prerequisite graphs and tech trees.
- Stages: concept → prototype → experimental → functional → commercial → mass adoption → mature → obsolescent → legacy.
- Adoption/accessibility/affordability/skills/education; regional inequality.
- Infrastructure dependency, household/communication/transport/medical/agricultural/manufacturing technology, automation and labor.
- AI can be a technology category if applicable.
- Culture/religion/government/military/double-use interactions.
- Secrecy, IP, transfer, standards, compatibility.
- Failures, risks, breakthroughs, historical eras, transitions, legacy technology.
- Technology interacts with migration, education, population, resources, environment, history.
- Distinct states: existence, knowledge, production, availability, affordability, accessibility, adoption.
- Player experience, resolution, validation.

Core principle: Technology is accumulated capability, not a civilization score; existence ≠ known ≠ produced ≠ affordable ≠ accessible ≠ adopted.
