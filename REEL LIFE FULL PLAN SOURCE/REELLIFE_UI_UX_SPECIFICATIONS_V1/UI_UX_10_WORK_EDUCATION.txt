REELLIFE — UI/UX SPECIFICATION V1
UI/UX 10 — Work & Education
==============================================================================

Purpose
Defines presentation of employment, labor, education, learning, credentials, schedules, and career progression.

Scope
This document describes presentation, interaction, navigation, information display, and UI boundaries. It does not own simulation truth.

1. Work View
------------
Show current employer, role, compensation, schedule, workplace, duties, conditions, commitments, performance context, and relevant opportunities.

2. Job Discovery
----------------
Search and browse vacancies according to location, qualifications, credentials, timing, and player knowledge.

3. Employment Actions
---------------------
Apply, accept, resign, negotiate, request leave, change schedule, transfer, train, and other commands as supported by authoritative systems.

4. Education View
-----------------
Show institution, program, course, attendance, assessments, progress, credentials, tuition/costs, schedule, and available learning actions.

5. Learning vs Credential
-------------------------
UI should clearly distinguish actual competence development from formal certification or credentials.

6. Time Integration
-------------------
Work and education should consume Activities and interact with schedules, travel, needs, household responsibilities, and commitments.

End of specification.
