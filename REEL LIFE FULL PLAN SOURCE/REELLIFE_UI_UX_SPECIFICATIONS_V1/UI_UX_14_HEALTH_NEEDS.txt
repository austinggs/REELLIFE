REELLIFE — UI/UX SPECIFICATION V1
UI/UX 14 — Health & Needs
==============================================================================

Purpose
Defines presentation of physical health, mental/emotional state, and daily needs without reducing the person to a collection of bars.

Scope
This document describes presentation, interaction, navigation, information display, and UI boundaries. It does not own simulation truth.

1. Needs
--------
Hunger, thirst, sleep/rest, temperature, hygiene, toilet, social/personal-space/recreation needs where enabled should be represented through meaningful status and context.

2. Health
---------
Conditions, injuries, symptoms, severity, treatment state, functional impact, and uncertainty should be distinguishable.

3. Diagnosis
------------
A diagnosis is an interpretation based on evidence, not automatically objective truth. UI should communicate diagnostic confidence or uncertainty where relevant.

4. Mental / Emotional
---------------------
Emotion, mood, stress, grief, coping, and appraisal should be presented in a way that communicates state without implying direct access to another person’s hidden mind unless authorized.

5. Care Actions
---------------
Appointments, medication, rest, food, exercise, treatment, emergency care, and other actions should be contextual commands with validation.

6. Privacy
----------
Sensitive health information must follow access rules. NPCs should not expose hidden medical details simply because the simulation knows them.

End of specification.
