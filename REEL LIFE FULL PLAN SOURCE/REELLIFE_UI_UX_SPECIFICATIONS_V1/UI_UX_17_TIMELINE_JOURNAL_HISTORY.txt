REELLIFE — UI/UX SPECIFICATION V1
UI/UX 17 — Timeline / Journal / History
==============================================================================

Purpose
Defines the player historical understanding of life and world events through a unified presentation layer.

Scope
This document describes presentation, interaction, navigation, information display, and UI boundaries. It does not own simulation truth.

1. Unified History Layer
------------------------
Timeline, Journal, and Statistics are presentation views built from event history and derived aggregates. None becomes authoritative simulation state.

2. Timeline
-----------
Chronological record of meaningful events with dates, locations, actors, and relationships.

3. Journal
----------
Player-perspective record emphasizing what the player experienced, noticed, recorded, or was told.

4. Statistics
-------------
Derived analytics such as lifetime earnings, jobs held, moves, education milestones, relationships, health milestones, and other meaningful aggregates.

5. Causal Inspection
--------------------
Where supported, a history item can open the event chain that produced a result. Hidden/private portions should remain access-controlled.

6. Filtering
------------
By time, person, place, event type, organization, relationship, importance, and knowledge state.

7. Legacy
---------
History persists across generations and should allow the player to understand how prior lives affected later ones without erasing private/unknown information boundaries.

End of specification.
