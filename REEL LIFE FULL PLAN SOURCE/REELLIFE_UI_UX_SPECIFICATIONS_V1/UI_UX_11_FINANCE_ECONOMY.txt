REELLIFE — UI/UX SPECIFICATION V1
UI/UX 11 — Finance & Economy
==============================================================================

Purpose
Defines understandable financial presentation while preserving precise underlying monetary state.

Scope
This document describes presentation, interaction, navigation, information display, and UI boundaries. It does not own simulation truth.

1. Financial Overview
---------------------
Show cash/liquid balances, accounts, income, expenses, debt, recurring obligations, assets, and significant upcoming financial events.

2. Precision
------------
Money is authoritative in integer minor units. UI formatting may round for readability only when the authoritative value remains accessible.

3. Transactions
---------------
Every monetary change should be traceable to a transaction/event with enough context to understand source, destination, purpose, and time.

4. Budget Context
-----------------
Budgeting is a presentation/decision aid. It should not replace the actual ledger or invent balances.

5. Markets
----------
Market information may include prices, trends, scarcity, competition, and availability. Estimates and forecasts must be labeled as such.

6. Contracts & Obligations
--------------------------
Leases, loans, subscriptions, insurance, employment compensation, and other obligations should present their terms and next consequences clearly.

7. Economic Scale
-----------------
Country and world economic views are aggregates and should not imply that the player can control macro outcomes directly.

End of specification.
