REELLIFE — UI/UX SPECIFICATION V1
UI/UX 15 — Organizations & Institutions
==============================================================================

Purpose
Defines interfaces for organizations, businesses, schools, hospitals, banks, governments, religious organizations, media, and other institutional structures.

Scope
This document describes presentation, interaction, navigation, information display, and UI boundaries. It does not own simulation truth.

1. Organization Overview
------------------------
Show identity, type, leadership, roles, locations, assets/liabilities, goals, policies, contracts, current state, reputation, and public information as appropriate.

2. Membership / Employment
--------------------------
Link people to organizations through roles, employment, membership, ownership, governance, or participation rather than treating all affiliation as one relationship type.

3. Institutional Memory
-----------------------
Where relevant, show records, policies, precedents, history, and public-facing institutional information. Private organizational knowledge follows access rules.

4. Capacity
-----------
Institutions may have limited capacity, funding, staff, infrastructure, and operational performance. UI should expose meaningful capacity constraints without creating fake precision.

5. Organizational Actions
-------------------------
Join, apply, hire, fire, promote, contract, purchase, sell, merge, communicate, or submit requests according to authority.

End of specification.
