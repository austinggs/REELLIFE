REELLIFE — SYSTEM 27: HOUSING & PROPERTY
==========================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models homes, rentals, ownership, leases, property condition, utilities, housing availability, and residence.

OWNS
-----
- property and unit state
- housing supply
- tenancy/residence
- leases
- maintenance/condition
- utilities at property context
- housing instability states
- housing history
- property-specific housing constraints

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- ownership rights semantics
- money ledger
- transport
- needs
- city geography truth

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Property includes location, type, structure, units, condition, utilities, ownership, tenancy, market context, and history
- Residence includes property/unit, occupants, start/end, and status
- Housing conditions include size, privacy, safety, sanitation, temperature, utilities, accessibility, and overcrowding

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Housing instability can be stable, temporary, overcrowded, threatened, evicted, homeless, or transitional
- Housing decisions depend on affordability, location, commute, family needs, preferences, availability, safety, reputation, and legal eligibility
- Maintenance depends on condition, resources, ownership/lease obligations, and service access

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Geography, Finance, Ownership, Family, Needs, Health, Employment, Transport, and Population
- Moves are Commands/Activities producing events and updating residence history

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test rent/mortgage pressure, vacancies, overcrowding, homelessness, eviction, shared housing, utility outages, and property deterioration

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Housing is a physical environment and resource relationship emerging from property, finance, geography, household, and choices.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
