REELLIFE — SYSTEM 32: ORGANIZATION CORE
=========================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Universal collective-actor foundation for companies, schools, hospitals, governments, charities, clubs, banks, media, unions, religious groups, and other organizations.

OWNS
-----
- organization identity
- type/legal identity
- parent/child hierarchy
- members and roles
- departments/units
- organization assets
- locations
- resources
- goals/policies
- authority model
- organization history
- basic organizational reputation/finance references

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- commercial strategy details
- specific institutional procedures
- individual employee behavior
- legal rules
- market mechanics

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Organization includes type/name/legal identity/parent/subsidiaries/members/roles/departments/assets/locations/resources/goals/policies/authority/reputation/finances/contracts/operations/history
- Membership roles can include employee, student, member, officer, manager, owner, volunteer, contractor, customer, or participant
- Hierarchy supports parent/division/department/team/unit

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Organization lifecycle can be forming, active, suspended, restructuring, closed, or dissolved
- Authority is evaluated through shared AuthorityCheck
- Organization behavior emerges from leadership, policies, resources, members, incentives, and external conditions rather than a magical collective personality

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Foundation for Education, Employment, Businesses, Institutions, Government, Security, International Organizations, Culture, Media, and Finance
- Individuals remain ordinary actors with their own cognition/goals/decisions

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test membership changes, nested organizations, role authority, closures, restructuring, resource constraints, and delegated decisions

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Organizations are persistent collective actors built from people, roles, resources, procedures, and authority—not super-NPCs.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
