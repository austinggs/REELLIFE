REELLIFE — SYSTEM 31: INSURANCE & RISK MANAGEMENT
===================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models risk transfer, pooling, coverage, underwriting, claims, premiums, deductibles, and payouts.

OWNS
-----
- policy state
- coverage/limits/deductibles
- premium state
- underwriting
- claims
- claim review
- payouts
- cancellation
- beneficiaries where relevant
- fraud suspicion/appeal state

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- world risk incidents
- asset truth
- medical truth
- legal rules
- financial ledger mechanics

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Policy contains insurer, policyholder, insured subject, coverage, limits, deductible, premium, exclusions, dates, status, beneficiaries, and claim history
- Claim lifecycle: incident -> claimed -> reviewing -> approved/denied -> paid, with disputed/fraud-suspected/appealed states

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Risk is generated by the world; insurance changes how financial consequences are distributed
- Premiums can depend on probability, severity, exposure, history, location, asset, coverage, insurer, market, and regulation
- Moral hazard may change incentives and therefore behavior
- Coverage exclusions and limits matter to payouts

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Finance, Ownership, Health, Vehicles, Housing, Businesses, Government/Law, and Events
- Claim incidents originate in other systems and are evaluated here

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test partial coverage, deductibles, exclusions, repeated claims, risk changes, cancellation, underwriting shifts, fraud signals, and payout accounting

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Risk belongs to the world; insurance redistributes the financial consequences of that risk.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
