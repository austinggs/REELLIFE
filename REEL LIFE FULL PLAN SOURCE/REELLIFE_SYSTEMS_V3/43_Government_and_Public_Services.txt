REELLIFE — SYSTEM 43: GOVERNMENT & PUBLIC SERVICES
====================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models public agencies, taxation, budgets, service provision, policy implementation, public records, welfare, regulation, and emergency coordination.

OWNS
-----
- government structures
- branches/agencies
- official roles
- budgets
- public service systems
- policy implementation state
- administrative capacity
- public records
- emergency coordination
- government history/legitimacy references

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- laws themselves
- individual tax balances
- citizen beliefs
- market prices
- all infrastructure operations

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Government includes country, branches, agencies, officials, budgets, policies, services, authority, legitimacy, administrative capacity, and history
- Service quality emerges from funding, capacity, staff, infrastructure, demand, geography, policy, administration, corruption/fraud, and emergencies

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Tax rules are defined by Laws; transactions are executed by Finance
- Policies change conditions and incentives rather than directly rewriting person state
- Government workers are ordinary people with competence, workload, information limits, incentives, and mistakes
- Emergency response coordinates across institutions and infrastructure

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Laws, Finance, Institutions, Organizations, Geography, Population, Infrastructure, Health, Security, and International Relations

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test budget changes, service overload, policy implementation gaps, administrative errors, emergency response, tax changes, and local service variation

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Government changes conditions through institutions, rules, budgets, services, and authority.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
