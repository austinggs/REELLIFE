REELLIFE — SYSTEM 11: PHYSICAL HEALTH & MEDICINE
==================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models physical conditions, injuries, symptoms, treatment, recovery, impairment, and physiological risk.

OWNS
-----
- health state
- conditions and injuries
- symptoms
- severity/progression
- treatments and medications
- allergies/contraindications
- risk factors and vulnerabilities
- functional capacity
- recovery states
- medical history

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- mental state
- hospital staffing/capacity
- insurance coverage
- transport availability
- medical pricing
- healthcare law/policy
- NPC treatment decisions

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- HealthState includes overallCondition, conditions, injuries, symptoms, treatments, meds, allergies, riskFactors, vulnerabilities, recoveryStates, history, functionalCapacity, vitalState, and death-risk context
- Conditions distinguish acute/chronic, onset, severity, status, cause, symptoms, progression, treatment, prognosis, function, and visibility
- Symptoms are evidence, not diagnoses

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Diagnosis is an interpretation rather than the underlying physical truth
- Detection may be incomplete or wrong
- Treatment can cure, improve, stabilize, slow, reduce, fail, or cause complications
- Adherence, contraindications, allergies, and interactions affect outcomes
- Functional capacity is continuous/contextual rather than boolean
- Death physiology feeds Life Continuity rather than being owned here

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Healthcare Institutions, Finance, Insurance, Transport, Employment, Geography, Needs, and Mental State
- Medical records are separate from body truth and may differ in visibility or completeness

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test onset, progression, recovery, treatment failure, uncertainty, access barriers, chronic conditions, interactions, and deterministic health risks

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Health describes physical condition and process; it does not automatically decide what care is accessible or what a person chooses.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
