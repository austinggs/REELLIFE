REELLIFE — MASTER SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE V3
==========================================
Status: Architectural lock draft for review

PURPOSE
-------
This document converts the approved conceptual Systems 01–74 register and the
user's review into the implementation-level architecture. It defines top-level
systems, merged systems, shared primitives, ownership boundaries, dependencies,
and deferred expansion areas.

CORE LAW
--------
CONDITIONS → DECISIONS → EVENTS → CONSEQUENCES → NEW CONDITIONS

ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

======================================================================
I. FOUNDATION
======================================================================

01 — Simulation Core & World State
Role: authoritative coordinator/infrastructure layer.
Owns: WorldState coordination, registries, lifecycle, indexes, validation,
system registration, simulation coordination.
Does not own domain decisions such as affordability, forgiveness, promotion,
medical judgment, or legal permission.

02 — Time, Calendar & Clock
Owns: authoritative timestamp, calendar, durations, time zones, local time,
seasons, holidays, day/night, deadlines, expiration, temporal ordering,
simulation speed/pause.
Boundary: Time = WHEN; Activities = WHAT IS EXPECTED; Decision system =
WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS.

03 — Randomness, Seeds & Scenario Resolution
Owns: master/world/scenario/entity seeds, RNG streams, distributions,
weighted outcomes, deterministic scenario resolution, replay metadata.
Rules: no authoritative Math.random(); eligibility is separate from
probability; impossible differs from unlikely; save/load preserves RNG state.

04 — Event & Consequence Engine
Action = attempt; Event = occurrence; Consequence = resulting change.
Owns event structure, ordering, handlers, causal links, delayed effects,
idempotency, cancellation, stale-event handling, event-storm prevention,
replay/inspection.
Domain systems own the actual state changes.

05 — Schedules, Activities & Commitments
Schedule = recurring/planned pattern.
Activity = time-occupying instance.
Commitment = activity with consequences for failure.
Owns schedules, activities, commitments, reservations, availability and
recurrence. Planned behavior is not guaranteed behavior.

06 — Persistence, Save/Load & Continuity
Native format: .reel.
Stores serializable authoritative world representation, entity IDs, system
state, clock, RNG, pending events, activities, required history,
configuration, schema/simulation/content/RNG/serialization versions and
integrity data.
Requires atomic writes, validation, recovery and explicit migrations.

07 — Simulation Scale, Relevance & Materialization
Resolution levels: Abstract, Light, Active, High Detail.
Relevance considers relationships, interactions, geography, institutions,
events, schedules, causal dependency and player connection.
Materialization reveals an existing life; it must not invent a convenient one.
Compression must preserve causality and determinism.

======================================================================
II. HUMAN
======================================================================

08 — Character Identity & Origin
Owns immutable personId, identity fields, origin, birth, parentage references,
appearance foundation, generation metadata and identity history.
Names can change. Death does not delete identity. Origin creates conditions,
not destiny. Player and NPCs use the same Person architecture.

09 — Aging, Development & Life Stages
Owns age/development facts, life-stage transitions and milestones.
Consumes Time, Health, Needs, Education, Skills, Family and environment.
Chronological age, biological/developmental state and legal age are distinct.

10 — Needs & Daily Living
Owns recurring need pressure/deficits and satisfaction state.
Examples: hunger, thirst, sleep, hygiene, comfort, safety, social contact,
rest and environmental comfort.
Does not own food, housing, health or psychology.

11 — Physical Health & Medicine
Owns conditions, injuries, symptoms, treatment and recovery state.
Does NOT own hospital capacity, staffing, insurance eligibility, transport
access or geography.

12 — Mental & Emotional State
Owns mood, emotional state, stress, resilience and configurable mental-health
state. Not every reaction is a clinical diagnosis.

13 — Personality, Traits & Aptitudes
Owns relatively persistent traits, preferences, aptitudes and tendencies.
Traits modify decisions; they do not dictate them.

14 — Skills & Competence
Owns learned capabilities, proficiency, practice and competence.
Skill is explicitly separate from credential.

15 — Cognition & Knowledge
MERGES original Memory + Knowledge/Beliefs/Perception.
Owns memory records, knowledge, beliefs, perception filters, source
confidence and recall/access state.
World truth != person knowledge. Information may be incomplete, false,
outdated, forgotten or misunderstood.

16 — Goals, Aspirations & Motivation
Owns goals, aspirations, priorities, motivation pressures and progress.
Goals are not quests and are not scripts.

17 — NPC Decision-Making & Autonomy
Central behavior system.
Consumes state, goals, personality, needs, knowledge, relationships,
schedules, obligations, resources, laws, opportunities and risks.
Produces commands/actions/plans. NPCs use the same action/event architecture
as the player.

======================================================================
III. SOCIAL
======================================================================

18 — Relationship & Social Interaction Core
MERGES Relationships + Conversation + Dating/Romance.
One universal relationship architecture with friendship, romance, family,
household, workplace, business, professional, community and institutional
contexts.
Conversation is an interaction mechanism; romance is a specialized context.

19 — Family, Household & Genealogy
Owns family links, household membership, genealogy and family roles.
Family != household != legal relation.

20 — Children & Parenting
SUBMODULE of Family/Household.
Owns caregiving obligations, parenting responsibilities and custody-related
state where applicable. It does not independently own child development,
health, needs or education.

21 — Conflict, Negotiation & Reconciliation
Owns disputes, agreements, negotiation, escalation/de-escalation and repair.
Interacts with relationships, contracts, organizations, law, courts and
reputation.

22 — Reputation & Social Perception
Owns audience-specific reputation, familiarity, social perception and fame.
Reputation is not a universal objective score; it depends on audience and
available information.

======================================================================
IV. EDUCATION / EMPLOYMENT
======================================================================

23 — Education
Owns enrollment, programs, academic progression, credentials, attendance and
educational records. Uses organizations, skills, aging, finance, transport
and schedules.

24 — Employment & Labor
MERGES Careers/Jobs + Workplace + Labor Market.
Submodules:
- Employment: positions, contracts, compensation.
- Workplace: organizational daily life.
- Labor Market: aggregate labor supply/demand.
Organization Core owns generic teams/hierarchy/policies.

======================================================================
V. FINANCE / PROPERTY / CONSUMPTION
======================================================================

25 — Finance & Economy Architecture
Unified architecture for:
- Personal/Household Finance: budgets, obligations, cash-flow pressure.
- Banking/Credit: accounts, loans, payments, credit profiles.
- Investment: holdings, positions, orders.
- Markets: prices, supply/demand, clearing.
- Macro: aggregate indicators and shocks.
Macro never directly rewrites personal state; effects propagate through jobs,
prices, credit, demand, supply and other channels.
Money uses integer minor units, never authoritative floating-point balances.

26 — Ownership / Contracts / Asset Rights
SHARED PRIMITIVE, not a normal standalone simulation brain.
Supports ownership, possession, custody, leases, titles, liens, contracts,
rights, obligations and transfers.
Used by housing, vehicles, businesses, banking, investment, estates,
insurance, organizations and government.

27 — Housing & Property
Domain module on top of Ownership + Markets + Geography.
Owns residence, occupancy, condition and housing-market interactions.

28 — Transportation & Vehicles
Owns vehicles, vehicle condition, transport modes, route/availability
interactions, travel feasibility and travel costs.
Travel takes time and can fail.

29 — Inventory / Items & Object State
Shared object domain.
Owns item identity, quantity, location, condition, ownership reference,
durability and spoilage where relevant.

30 — Food / Nutrition & Consumption
Owns food, meals, nutrition, spoilage, consumption and food security.
Built on Items + Needs + Health + Markets + Finance.

31 — Insurance & Risk Management
New formalized system.
Owns policies, premiums, coverage, deductibles, claims, payouts,
exclusions and policy state.
Domains: health, vehicle, property, business, life and other insurable risks.

======================================================================
VI. ORGANIZATIONS / MARKETS
======================================================================

32 — Organization Core
Universal organization model for businesses, corporations, schools,
hospitals, government agencies, courts, police, clubs, religious groups,
communities, criminal groups and hidden organizations.
Owns identity, membership, roles, leadership, departments, hierarchy,
facilities, resources, policies, schedules and internal organization.

33 — Organizations & Businesses
MERGES Businesses + Corporations + Organizational Control.
Submodules: legal structure, ownership/control, operations, B2B and supply
chain. Uses Organization Core, Ownership, Contracts, Employment, Markets,
Finance and Information.

34 — Supply Chains & B2B
Submodule/domain under Organizations & Businesses.
Owns supplier/customer relationships, procurement, production dependencies,
logistics dependencies, inventory flow and B2B contracts.

35 — Markets / Prices & Competition
Owns supply, demand, price formation, market clearing and competition state.
Businesses submit offers/actions; markets resolve market state.

36 — Macroeconomic Layer
Owns aggregate economic indicators, cycles and shocks.
Does not directly mutate individuals.

======================================================================
VII. WORLD / SOCIETY
======================================================================

37 — Geography / Cities / Neighborhoods / Infrastructure
Spatial authority for world geography, cities, neighborhoods, buildings,
spatial relationships and infrastructure references.

38 — Infrastructure Operations
New formalized system.
Owns maintenance, outages, capacity, degradation, repairs, construction
status and service availability for roads, power, water, communications,
transport infrastructure and public facilities.

39 — Countries & World Rules
Owns country identity, jurisdictional metadata and broad data-driven rule
configuration.

40 — Legal Identity & Administrative Records
Owns official identity records, citizenship, registrations, licenses,
permits, documents and administrative status.

41 — Laws & Regulatory Rules
Owns laws, regulations, jurisdictions, effective periods, applicability,
rights, penalties and restrictions.

42 — Institutions & Institutional Memory
Owns institutional procedures, workflows, case history, internal records,
policies and long-term institutional memory.

43 — Government & Public Services
Owns public budgets, services, programs and policy execution.
Uses Organization Core and shared legal/administrative infrastructure.

44 — Culture / Religion / Community / Tradition
Owns cultural norms, traditions, community practices and religious contexts.
Specialized religious organizations use Organization Core.

45 — Travel / Immigration / Borders
Owns travel rules, border controls, visas, residency and migration
processes. Uses Legal Identity, Countries, Authority Checks, Transport,
Schedules and Geography.

46 — Weather / Environment / Disasters
Owns weather, environmental conditions, hazards and disasters and their
environmental effects.

47 — Population & Demographics
New aggregate layer.
Owns population groups, demographic rates, births, deaths, migration,
household formation/dissolution, age distributions and broad population
changes.
Must support deterministic individual materialization from aggregate state.

======================================================================
VIII. SECURITY / LEGAL
======================================================================

48 — Security & Legal Pipeline
MERGES Crime/Emergencies + Justice/Courts + Criminal Organizations +
Hidden/Grey/Black Economies.
Layers:
- Incident/Emergency
- Investigation/Evidence
- Court/Proceeding
- Criminal/Hidden Organization
- Secrecy/Detection
Criminal organizations use Organization Core. Hiddenness uses Authority,
Access and Secrecy primitives.

======================================================================
IX. INFORMATION
======================================================================

49 — Information / Communication / Media
MERGES Information Graph + News/Media + Internet/Social Media.
Submodules:
- Information Graph: claims, sources, provenance, confidence, propagation.
- Communication: in-person, phone, mail, digital and institutional channels.
- Media: editorial/newsroom behavior.
- Internet/Social: platforms, accounts, posts, algorithms.
Information truth and personal knowledge remain distinct.

50 — Communication / Messaging
SUBLAYER of 49.
Owns messages, sender/recipient, channel, timestamp, delivery and
visibility/access state.
Channels include in-person, phone, mail, text, email, social and institutional.

======================================================================
X. TECHNOLOGY / GLOBAL
======================================================================

51 — Technology & Historical Change
Owns technologies, availability, adoption, diffusion and technological eras.
Affects jobs, business, medicine, transport, communication, education,
security and household life.

52 — International Relations & Global Events
Owns diplomatic relationships, treaties, international events, sanctions,
conflicts and international trade relationships where modeled.
Keep abstract early; increase detail when relevant.

======================================================================
XI. LIFE CONTINUITY
======================================================================

53 — Life Continuity
MERGES Death + Estate + Generations + Multi-Generational Consequences.
Pipeline:
Death → End-of-Life/Funeral → Estate/Probate → Succession →
Generational Continuation → Legacy/Consequence Tracing.
Legacy must travel through actual property, records, institutions,
relationships, culture, knowledge, organizations or reputation.
No arbitrary invisible descendant bonuses/penalties.

======================================================================
XII. PLAYER / SUPPORT
======================================================================

54 — Player History & Analytics
MERGES Timeline + Journal + Statistics.
Timeline = curated chronology.
Journal = player-perspective record.
Statistics = derived analytics.
None becomes authoritative simulation state.

55 — Player UI
Presentation layer only. Reads state and issues commands.

56 — Map & Spatial Presentation
Presentation layer for geography and spatial state. Does not own spatial truth.

57 — Console / Command Interface
Debug/power-user interface using command/query architecture.
May expose hidden/debug state only in authorized modes.

58 — Configuration & Content Architecture
MERGES Game Modes + Difficulty + Data/Rules.
Schemas for scenario, difficulty, world configuration, content definitions,
rule configuration and mod/expansion metadata.

59 — Debugging / Observability / Simulation Testing
Required infrastructure.
Owns diagnostics, traces, event inspection, causal inspection, invariants,
performance telemetry, simulation probes and cross-system tests.
Must support deterministic replay and save/load equivalence testing.

======================================================================
SHARED PRIMITIVES
======================================================================

EntityRef
- Stable entity reference surviving save/load and lifecycle changes.

Money
- Integer minor units; never authoritative floating-point balances.

Time
- Timestamp/duration representation tied to the authoritative clock.

LocationRef
- Stable spatial reference.

EventRef
- Stable event reference.

Contract
- Parties, rights, obligations, terms, dates and state.

Relationship
- Universal contextual interpersonal relationship structure.

Organization
- Universal organization structure.

InformationClaim
- Claim + source/provenance + confidence + visibility + propagation state.

Asset
- Economic/legal/ownership-relevant thing.

Activity
- Time-occupying action instance.

AuthorityCheck
- actor + action + target + jurisdiction + time + context →
  ALLOWED / DENIED / CONDITIONAL.

ResolutionObject
- applicability + conditions + candidates + weights/probabilities +
  selected outcome + RNG/debug metadata.

======================================================================
ACTION / COMMAND MODEL
======================================================================

This is a required shared architecture primitive.

Pipeline:
Intent → Command → Validation → Action Resolution → Event →
Consequences → New State

Example:
Player wants to quit a job
→ QuitJobCommand
→ validate contract/authority/time
→ employment resolves resignation
→ JobResignation event
→ finance + schedule + reputation + organization + information effects

The UI never performs those mutations itself.

======================================================================
AUTHORITY / ACCESS MODEL
======================================================================

Reusable evaluation:
actor + action + target + jurisdiction + time + role + credentials +
ownership + permissions + legal status + organizational status +
secrecy/access state
→ allowed / denied / conditional

Examples:
- entering a building
- accessing records
- voting
- crossing a border
- accessing evidence
- performing regulated work
- transferring an asset

Law defines rules; AuthorityCheck resolves a specific contextual action.

======================================================================
POPULATION / ABSTRACT SIMULATION RULES
======================================================================

At low resolution, simulate groups:
births, deaths, age distribution, households, migration, employment,
education participation, broad health, housing pressure and movement.

When relevance increases, materialize individuals.

Materialization must preserve:
existing demographic history, geography, economic conditions, relevant
relationships and causal history.

Never create a convenient NPC merely because the player looks at them.

======================================================================
INFRASTRUCTURE / CONSTRUCTION EXPANSION
======================================================================

Construction/Industrial Capacity is a planned later expansion, not required
for the initial core.

Potential state:
construction projects, industrial capacity, production facilities,
materials, construction labor, capital requirements and infrastructure
buildout.

It integrates with Organizations, Markets, Finance, Employment, Geography,
Infrastructure and Technology.

FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
----------------------
ReelLife should feel alive because interacting systems generate stories,
not because stories are secretly scripted.

People have state.
People have needs.
People have knowledge.
People have relationships.
People make decisions.
Organizations have incentives.
Institutions have procedures.
Markets respond.
Laws constrain behavior.
Information propagates.
Time passes.
Environments change.
Events create consequences.
Consequences create new conditions.

CONDITIONS
    ↓
DECISIONS
    ↓
EVENTS
    ↓
CONSEQUENCES
    ↓
NEW CONDITIONS
    ↓
MORE DECISIONS
    ↓
MORE EVENTS
    ↓
...

That loop is ReelLife.
