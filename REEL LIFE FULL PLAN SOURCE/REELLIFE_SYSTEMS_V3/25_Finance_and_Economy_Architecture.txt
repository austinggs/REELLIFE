REELLIFE — SYSTEM 25: FINANCE & ECONOMY ARCHITECTURE
======================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Provides precise monetary state and economic flows for people, households, businesses, institutions, and governments.

OWNS
-----
- Money primitive
- accounts and ledgers
- income/expense flows
- debt/credit state
- savings
- investment positions
- financial obligations
- interest and fees where modeled
- economic transaction history
- aggregate economic flow integration

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- physical assets themselves
- market structure alone
- legal rules alone
- UI representations of balances

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Money uses currency + integer minorUnits; authoritative balances never rely on floating-point money
- Ledger-based state makes monetary changes traceable
- Account types include cash, bank, savings, credit, loans, investments, and business/institutional accounts
- Credit reflects repayment history, income, assets, obligations, risk, collateral, and policy

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Every authoritative monetary change has a causal trace
- Investment includes ownership, securities/funds/business investment, returns, losses, dividends, and capital gains; detailed market mechanics are System 35
- Economy loop: production -> income -> consumption -> savings -> investment -> credit -> prices -> demand/supply -> business -> employment
- Personal finance remains contextual to household/business obligations rather than a UI budget meter

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Feeds Employment, Markets, Housing, Businesses, Insurance, Government, Macro, Ownership, and Life Continuity
- Taxes/rules come from Laws; monetary transfers execute through the ledger
- Ownership of assets is defined by System 26

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test conservation/traceability, debt accrual, defaults, transfers, inheritance, business cash flow, currency differences, and precision boundaries

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Every authoritative monetary change must have a believable economic cause and a traceable ledger path.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
