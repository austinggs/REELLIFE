REELLIFE — SYSTEM 34: SUPPLY CHAINS & B2B
===========================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models procurement, supplier relationships, purchase orders, logistics dependencies, wholesale flows, and disruption cascades.

OWNS
-----
- procurement
- supplier relationships
- purchase orders
- supply dependencies
- B2B exchange records
- logistics dependency state
- supplier capacity/lead times
- substitution options
- supply-chain history

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- overall market price theory
- business ownership
- transport network truth
- generic legal contract framework

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Supply loop: demand -> procurement -> suppliers -> inputs -> operations -> output -> distribution -> customers
- PurchaseOrder captures buyer/supplier/item/quantity/price/timing/status
- SupplyDependency links required input/capability to supplier or alternative source

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Cascades should emerge from dependency networks rather than scripted sequences
- Substitution depends on price, quality, reliability, geography, compatibility, reputation, switching cost, and availability
- Supplier concentration increases exposure to correlated disruptions
- Lead times and logistics create lagged effects

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Organizations, Finance, Markets, Inventory, Transportation, Infrastructure, Technology, Disasters, and Law/Contracts

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test supplier failure, port/road disruption, substitute sourcing, delayed inputs, cascading shortages, contract breaches, and demand shocks

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Supply chains are networks of dependencies, not linear scripts.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
