REELLIFE — SYSTEM 20: CHILDREN & PARENTING
============================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models the ongoing care, supervision, teaching, protection, discipline, support, and autonomy of children.

OWNS
-----
- parenting relationships/processes
- caregiving
- supervision
- support
- guidance
- discipline
- protection
- child autonomy
- care coordination
- parent involvement/history
- parental decisions

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- parentage
- personality/development outcomes
- education institutions
- health truth
- household membership
- finance ledgers
- NPC decision architecture

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- ParentingRelationship tracks caregiver type, responsibilities, involvement, expectations, knowledge, resources, and autonomy support
- Caregivers can be parents, guardians, foster caregivers, relatives, institutions, or other configured roles
- Independence develops across practical, social, financial, emotional, and decision dimensions

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Parenting consumes time, money, energy, attention, and resources
- Care quality depends on child needs, caregiver availability, resources, knowledge, health, household conditions, priorities, and institutions
- Parents do not know everything and can make mistakes
- Discipline is modeled broadly and contextually rather than as a single moralized style switch
- Children increasingly participate in decisions as capacity develops

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Needs, Aging, Health, Mental State, Education, Family, Employment, Housing, Finance, Schedules, and NPC Decision
- Childcare activities can be represented with the shared Activity system

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test caregiver scarcity, conflicting caregiver responsibilities, emergencies, mistakes, changing autonomy, resource deprivation, and multiple caregivers

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Parents influence children through ongoing conditions and interactions; they do not author children into predetermined outcomes.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
