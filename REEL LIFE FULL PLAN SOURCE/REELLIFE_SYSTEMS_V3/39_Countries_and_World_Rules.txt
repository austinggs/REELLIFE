REELLIFE — SYSTEM 39: COUNTRIES & WORLD RULES
===============================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models broad jurisdictional and national environments that configure legal, economic, civic, border, and institutional conditions.

OWNS
-----
- country/territory identity
- sovereignty references
- jurisdiction hierarchy
- currency references
- national institutions
- border configuration
- citizenship framework
- immigration framework
- broad economic/cultural configuration
- country history

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- specific legal rules
- individual legal identity
- foreign relations details
- city geography
- individual culture beliefs

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Country includes territory, government, legal system, currency, jurisdictions, citizenship, immigration, economy, culture, institutions, infrastructure, and history
- Jurisdictions can overlap or nest
- Rules can vary across time

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Borders affect movement, trade, legal status, security, migration, and information
- Historical configurations support era-specific world rules
- Country-level settings should be data-driven rather than scattered magic constants

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Feeds Legal Identity, Laws, Government, Travel/Immigration, International Relations, Finance/Currency, Culture, Population, and Geography

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test historical rule changes, multiple jurisdictions, cross-border interactions, currency differences, territories, and citizenship edge cases

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Countries are rule-and-institution environments, not just polygons on a map.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
