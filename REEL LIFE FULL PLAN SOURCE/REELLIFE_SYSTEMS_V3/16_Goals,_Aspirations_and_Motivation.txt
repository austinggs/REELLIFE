REELLIFE — SYSTEM 16: GOALS, ASPIRATIONS & MOTIVATION
=======================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models what people want, why they want it, how strongly they pursue it, and how goals adapt.

OWNS
-----
- goal objects
- goal hierarchy
- motivation sources
- priority and urgency
- commitment
- progress
- subgoals/dependencies
- goal status/history
- goal replacement/adaptation

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- needs themselves
- personality traits
- belief contents
- final decisions
- schedules
- legal permissions

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Goals can be immediate, short-term, long-term, or life-scale
- Motivation sources include needs, security, affection, belonging, achievement, curiosity, autonomy, status, reward, responsibility, values, fear, opportunity, social expectations, identity, habit, and obligation
- Goal status can be active, paused, completed, failed, abandoned, expired, replaced, or impossible

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Priority is not the same as urgency
- Goals can conflict and compete for limited time/resources
- Commitment changes with context
- Feasibility depends on actual conditions and perceived conditions
- Opportunities can unlock new trajectories
- Goals guide decisions but never hard-script them

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- NPC Decision evaluates goals alongside needs, relationships, habits, opportunities, risk, and constraints
- Education, Employment, Finance, Family, and social systems can create or remove opportunities
- Cognition and Mental State can change goal interpretation and persistence

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test conflicting goals, blocked goals, impossible goals, shifting priorities, long-term tradeoffs, and resolution compression

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Goals provide direction, not guaranteed behavior.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
