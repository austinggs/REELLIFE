REELLIFE — SYSTEM 09: AGING, DEVELOPMENT & LIFE STAGES
========================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Tracks how long a person has lived and how development changes across life.

OWNS
-----
- chronological age
- biological/developmental state where modeled
- life-stage state
- development milestones
- growth state
- dependency state
- stage history

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- medical diagnoses
- skill progression
- personality
- education outcomes
- legal age rules
- employment
- parenting behavior

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- DevelopmentState includes birthTimestamp, chronologicalAge, currentLifeStage, developmentalState, milestone history, growth state, threshold history, biological-development state, dependency state, and stageStart
- Life stages are data-driven and may include infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, later life, etc.
- Development dimensions can include physical, cognitive, emotional, social, and independence

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Chronological age is derived from Time rather than independently ticked
- Milestones can be age-associated or condition-dependent
- Development is influenced by baseline, biology, health, nutrition, caregiving, education, stress, social interaction, opportunity, traits, and environment
- Legal age and developmental age are separate
- Dependency and independence are multidimensional

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Time and interacts with Needs, Health, Family, Education, Skills, environment, and Life Continuity
- Threshold events can be AgeThreshold, LifeStageChanged, DevelopmentalMilestone, Transition, or DependencyChanged

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test age boundaries, skipped-time updates, milestone conditions, historical variation, dependency transitions, and resolution-tier consistency

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Aging says how long someone has lived and how development progressed; it does not define who they become.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
