REELLIFE — SYSTEM 44: CULTURE / RELIGION / COMMUNITY / TRADITION
==================================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models socially transmitted values, practices, identities, rituals, norms, symbols, communities, and traditions.

OWNS
-----
- cultural group definitions
- traditions/norms/languages/values/rituals/symbols
- community membership/participation
- transmission pathways
- institutional/community history
- religious/community organizational links

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- individual beliefs
- personality
- legal rules
- relationship quality
- information transport

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Cultural groups can be geographic, religious, professional, hobby-based, online, identity-based, or organizational
- Individual participation can be strong, moderate, weak, mixed, changing, or situational
- Religion separates identity, belief, practice, community, and institutions

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Transmission occurs through family, education, community, religion, media, migration, friends, work, institutions, and imitation
- Culture influences decisions and expression without determining behavior
- Traditions can change, mix, weaken, spread, or be contested

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Feeds Cognition, Personality expression, Relationships, Food, Education, Employment, Politics/government services, Media, Migration, and Community life

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test cultural mixing, migration, generational change, participation differences, norm conflict, religious practice variation, and tradition persistence

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Culture is a distributed social environment, not a personality preset.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
