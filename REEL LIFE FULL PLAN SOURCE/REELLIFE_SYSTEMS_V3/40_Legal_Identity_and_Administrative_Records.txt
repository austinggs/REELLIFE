REELLIFE — SYSTEM 40: LEGAL IDENTITY & ADMINISTRATIVE RECORDS
===============================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models formal institutional representations of people through documents, registrations, certificates, licenses, permits, and administrative records.

OWNS
-----
- legal/admin identity references
- birth/death registration
- names as recorded
- citizenship/residency records
- passports/IDs/licenses/permits
- certificates
- administrative records
- record corrections/disputes/access state

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- PersonId itself
- legal rules
- physical person truth
- criminal adjudication
- immigration policy itself

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- AdministrativeRecord includes authority, subject, type, issue/status/validity, source, corrections, and access rules
- Documents track issue, expiration, issuer, identifier/security metadata, status, and replacement history
- Legal status can include citizen, resident, visitor, undocumented, stateless, asylum, and scenario-specific states

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Records may be missing, delayed, wrong, conflicting, fraudulent, forged, or disputed
- Institutional records are representations and can differ from underlying world truth
- Document access is authority/permission sensitive

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Countries, Laws, Government, Travel/Immigration, Institutions, Security, Family, Life Continuity, and AuthorityCheck

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test missing records, duplicate identities, correction history, forgery, conflicting records, expiration, citizenship changes, and access restrictions

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Legal identity is an institutional representation of a person, not the person themselves.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
