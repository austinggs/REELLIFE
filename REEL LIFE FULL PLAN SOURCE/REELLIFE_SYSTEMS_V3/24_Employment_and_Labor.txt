REELLIFE — SYSTEM 24: EMPLOYMENT & LABOR
==========================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models work, jobs, employers, labor markets, wages, working conditions, career transitions, and unemployment.

OWNS
-----
- jobs and vacancies
- employment relationships
- job applications
- hiring/termination/resignation
- compensation/benefits
- working conditions
- promotions/transfers
- occupations
- labor supply/demand
- freelance/self-employment/apprenticeships
- employment history

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- skill truth
- business ownership
- finance ledger semantics
- personality
- legal rules
- reputation
- NPC choices

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Employment includes person, organization, job, start/end, status, compensation, benefits, working conditions, authority, contract, and history
- Labor markets respond to business demand, supply, wages, skills, geography, education, technology, migration, regulation, and market conditions
- Performance is contextual

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Hiring combines eligibility, credentials, skills, reputation, information, fit, availability, and employer constraints
- Performance combines competence, health, mental state, fatigue, equipment, task difficulty, preparation, workplace conditions, and uncertainty
- Termination/resignation generates explicit downstream events
- Unemployment is a state/condition that can affect finance, reputation, goals, household life, and mental state

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Strongly coupled to Organizations, Finance, Skills, Education, Schedules, Geography, Transport, Reputation, and NPC Decision

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test hiring, job loss, multiple jobs, promotions, discrimination/configured institutional rules, labor shocks, automation, migration, and work interruptions

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Employment creates opportunities and constraints for work; it does not determine competence or life trajectory.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
