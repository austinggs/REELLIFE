REELLIFE — SYSTEM 35: MARKETS / PRICES & COMPETITION
======================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models exchange environments, price formation, competition, scarcity, consumer choice, and market structure.

OWNS
-----
- market state
- price formation inputs
- supply/demand context
- competition structure
- market participants
- market transactions/quotes where modeled
- local/informal market variants

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- money ledger semantics
- individual utility calculation
- business ownership
- macroeconomic aggregates

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Prices reflect supply, demand, inventory, cost, competition, scarcity, expectations, transport, taxes, regulation, and market structure
- Structures can be competitive, monopoly, oligopoly, regulated, local, or informal
- Consumers use NPC Decision rather than a universal market score

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Information is imperfect
- Alternatives, urgency, quality, availability, reputation, and affordability affect choices
- Competition can occur on price, quality, location, convenience, reputation, innovation, specialization, capacity, and advertising
- Markets can remain locally fragmented

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Consumes Finance, Organizations, Supply Chains, Macro, Geography, Technology, Information, and Government/Law
- Feeds prices and availability back into household and business decisions

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test shortages, surpluses, monopolies, price shocks, differentiated goods, local markets, regulation, imperfect information, and competitor entry/exit

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Markets coordinate distributed decisions under incomplete information and constraints.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
