REELLIFE — SYSTEM 19: FAMILY, HOUSEHOLD & GENEALOGY
=====================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models family structure, household membership, lineage, caregiving structure, and shared living arrangements.

OWNS
-----
- genealogy
- parent-child structural links
- biological/legal/adoptive links
- household membership/history
- household roles
- dependents
- caregiver structure
- household formation/dissolution
- lineage and ancestry/descendants
- family records

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- affection/trust quality
- property title
- financial balances
- employment
- medical state
- NPC decisions
- inheritance settlement

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Family = structural biological/legal relationships
- Household = shared living/resource arrangement
- Household roles include adult, child, dependent, caregiver, head, spouse, roommate, boarder, foster
- Membership history records entry/exit rather than treating household as timeless

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Parentage is explicit and historically preserved
- Household and family may differ
- Shared resources remain contextual and are not a duplicate finance system
- Children are ordinary Persons; parenting behavior is System 20
- Marriage/partnership history can be represented structurally without defining emotional quality

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Feeds Aging, Parenting, Needs, Housing, Employment, Finance, Life Continuity, Relationships, and Schedules
- Events/commands handle formation, dissolution, birth, adoption, household moves, custody-like changes where configured

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test multi-household families, adoption/guardian structures, household turnover, dependents, caregiver changes, divorce/partnership history, and lineage continuity

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Family is structural lineage and kinship; household is a living arrangement; relationship is social meaning.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
