REELLIFE — SYSTEM 33: ORGANIZATIONS & BUSINESSES
==================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Commercial specialization of Organization Core for firms, stores, services, factories, startups, employers, and other businesses.

OWNS
-----
- business state
- commercial structure
- ownership references
- products/services
- locations
- employees
- suppliers/customers references
- assets/capacity
- revenue/cost flow references
- inventory
- strategy/operations
- business history

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- generic organization membership rules
- market price formation
- detailed industrial capacity
- ownership rights semantics
- personal labor competence

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Businesses can be sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, stores, services, factories, startups, and similar structures
- Business lifecycle: idea -> forming -> opening -> active -> expanding -> restructuring -> suspended -> closed
- Capacity is constrained by employees, equipment, space, capital, inventory, supply, and time

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Creation can come from entrepreneurship, investment, inheritance, partnerships, or spin-offs
- Management is driven by people and organizational decision processes
- Revenue can come from sales, contracts, subscriptions, services, licensing, or investment
- Costs include wages, rent, materials, utilities, transport, taxes, financing, insurance, maintenance, and marketing
- Failure can come from insolvency, low demand, supply shocks, competition, poor management, disasters, regulation, staff loss, financing, or reputation

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Finance, Employment, Supply Chains, Markets, Ownership, Insurance, Infrastructure, Law, Geography, and Reputation
- Industrial detail beyond abstract input/output is deferred to the planned construction/industrial expansion

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test startup creation, staffing, cash flow, supply failure, demand shocks, closures, restructures, spin-offs, ownership changes, and organizational autonomy

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
A business coordinates people, resources, capital, production, and exchange under uncertainty.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
