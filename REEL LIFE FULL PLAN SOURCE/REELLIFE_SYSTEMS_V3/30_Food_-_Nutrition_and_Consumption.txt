REELLIFE — SYSTEM 30: FOOD / NUTRITION & CONSUMPTION
======================================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Models food as a physical, nutritional, economic, cultural, and consumption system.

OWNS
-----
- food properties
- meals
- preparation
- spoilage
- dietary properties
- nutrition
- food safety
- consumption process
- food preference effects
- food origin/context

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- hunger need state
- general inventory ownership
- money ledger
- housing kitchen structure
- market prices

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- FoodItem can track quantity, freshness, spoilage, preparation, nutrition, safety, and origin
- Nutrition can include energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber, micronutrients, and hydration
- Process: acquire -> store -> prepare -> consume -> digestion/metabolic effects -> need satisfaction/nutrition effects

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Preparation can change safety, nutrition, taste, and shelf life
- Spoilage depends on time, temperature, storage, packaging, and preservation
- Food preference can be affected by culture, religion, family, availability, personal preference, and finance
- Hunger is a Needs input; food is not itself a need gauge

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Uses Inventory, Needs, Health, Culture, Housing, Finance, Markets, and NPC Decision
- Consumption creates events and monetary/inventory changes where appropriate

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test spoilage, preparation variants, dietary restrictions, shortages, refrigeration failures, cultural patterns, and nutrition over time

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Food is a physical resource connecting hunger, health, culture, finance, inventory, housing, and behavior.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
