REELLIFE — SYSTEM 41: LAWS & REGULATORY RULES
===============================================
Status: Approved architecture

ROLE
----
Provides machine-readable rules for permissions, prohibitions, obligations, licenses, penalties, exemptions, and regulatory requirements.

OWNS
-----
- rule definitions
- jurisdiction/effective-date scope
- conditions
- permissions/prohibitions/obligations
- penalties
- exemptions
- licensing/permit/inspection rules
- regulatory authorities
- historical rule versions

DOES NOT OWN
------------
- enforcement outcomes
- individual beliefs about laws
- court adjudication
- raw behavior
- organization authority beyond legal rules

MODEL / STATE
-------------
- Rule includes jurisdiction, effective dates, subjects, conditions, prohibitions, permissions, obligations, penalties, exemptions, and enforcement authority
- Law pipeline: law -> legal status -> AuthorityCheck -> detection -> enforcement -> adjudication

RULES / BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- Historical laws can change over time
- People may misunderstand, intentionally violate, exploit ambiguity, or lack access to legal information
- Law and enforcement are distinct
- Licensing and regulated-work requirements are contextual and jurisdiction-specific

SYSTEM INTERACTIONS
-------------------
- Feeds AuthorityCheck, Security/Legal, Organizations, Government, Travel, Finance, Employment, and Ownership
- Cognition/Information determine what people believe the law says

TESTING / QUALITY
-----------------
- Test effective-date changes, overlapping jurisdictions, conflicting rules, exemptions, permits, ambiguous cases, and enforcement separation

SHARED ARCHITECTURAL LAWS
--------------------------
1. One authoritative owner for every persistent state field.
2. Commands express intent; events represent occurrences; consequences change state.
3. UI never directly mutates simulation state.
4. Information/knowledge is distinct from world truth.
5. Legal identity is distinct from simulation identity.
6. Organization and relationship structures are universal, not duplicated per domain.
7. Time has one authoritative clock.
8. Authoritative RNG is seeded, serialized, and reproducible.
9. Derived state remains derived.
10. Simulation resolution may change detail, never causal truth.
11. Origin influences conditions/opportunities, not destiny.
12. Legacy propagates through concrete systems, not arbitrary hidden modifiers.
13. .reel is the native persistent world/save format.

OUTCOME / CAUSALITY CHECK
--------------------------
This system must participate in the governing loop: CONDITIONS -> DECISIONS -> EVENTS -> CONSEQUENCES -> NEW CONDITIONS.

CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------
Law defines formal rules; it does not define what people will do or what authorities will discover.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
Keep authoritative state single-owned, serializable, deterministic where configured, and separated from UI presentation. Cross-system effects should travel through Commands, Events, and explicit consequence handlers.

END OF SYSTEM SPEC
