WORLD BUILD 14 — WORLD BIBLE MASTER REFERENCE
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Purpose
-------
This is the authoritative first-pass master reference for the fictional world of ReelLife.

The governing rule is:
World Bible = world definition + initial world conditions.
Simulation = ongoing change and consequences.

Global population: approximately 6.8 billion
Continents: 6
Major oceans: 5

9. Cultures and Languages
-------------------------
Culture is learned, transmitted, adapted and contested.

Six explicitly established major language families:
Ardanic, Veyric, Sahric, Orinthian, Kharic, Ilyran.

Additional language states include minority, local, indigenous, creole, trade, sign, endangered, revived and constructed languages.

Culture and language may cross political borders.

The system also includes local traditions, denominations, syncretic communities, spiritual practices, secular worldviews and nonbelief.

11. Economy and Resources
------------------------
Mixed economy with private businesses, family businesses, cooperatives, state-owned enterprises, nonprofits, public institutions, informal businesses, agrarian households, multinational corporations and independent workers.

Major resource groups:
energy, metals/minerals, construction materials, biological resources, water.

Major corridors:
Ardan, Veyran, Saren, Orin, Kharic and Ilyran Maritime.

Major financial centers:
Arden, Veyr, Westhaven, Selin, Ilyra City.

12. Institutions and Organizations
----------------------------------
Aurelia contains governments, courts, schools, universities, hospitals, banks, companies, unions, religious organizations, media organizations, NGOs/civil society and international institutions.

Organizations use the universal Organization model. Institutions track rules, norms, authorities, participants, capacity, trust and historical change.

13. Technology
--------------
Contemporary Age with advanced computing, internet, mobile systems, cloud infrastructure, AI, robotics, biotechnology, modern medicine, aviation, modern vehicles, advanced manufacturing, satellites, renewable energy and nuclear power.

Technology must be separated into existence, availability, affordability and adoption.

14. Population
--------------
Approximately 6.8 billion people at game start.
Approximately 70% urban/metropolitan.
Population is dynamic through births, deaths, migration, household formation, aging and demographic shocks.

Aggregate populations can be materialized into relevant households and individuals while preserving causal demographic state.

15. Current World State
-----------------------
Game starts in a functioning but interconnected world containing:
- energy transition
- technology and AI expansion
- migration pressures
- water stress
- disaster recovery
- mining expansion
- port competition and infrastructure growth
- supply-chain pressure
- environmental challenges
- active international disputes
- political transitions
- uneven institutional capacity

The world is not at predetermined global war and no future event is scripted to a fixed outcome.

16. Game-Start Timeline
-----------------------
Relevant recent history begins in 2041.
Active and pending events exist at 1 January 2042, including regional infrastructure projects, Saren Basin flood recovery, technological and AI-sector expansion, water pressure in Sahraen, Kharic mining expansion, Westhaven port expansion, diplomatic negotiations, corporate restructuring, migration and international disputes.

17. Information and Knowledge
-----------------------------
Reality -> Observation -> Information -> Transmission -> Interpretation -> Belief -> Behavior

Information is not truth. Reputation is not truth. A widely believed claim is not automatically true. The simulation tracks public, restricted, private, secret and unknown knowledge.

18. Canonicality Rules
----------------------
When later content conflicts with the World Bible:
1. identify the conflict
2. determine source authority
3. preserve causal consistency
4. update dependent data
5. record the change

20. Definition vs State
-----------------------
World Definition = what exists.
World State = what is happening now.

This separation supports:
- standard mode
- historical mode
- alternate mode
- sandbox mode
- scenario mode
- custom mode

21. Expansion Rules
-------------------
New content should respect geography, history, demographics, culture, language, religion, economy, institutions, technology and existing political structures.

Do not add content only because a feature needs it. New content must fit the causal world.

This loop is the intended bridge between world content and ReelLife's simulation engine.

24. Final Principle
-------------------
Aurelia has history without a predetermined story.

The World Bible establishes enough structure that the simulation can answer where people live, why cities and industries exist, why cultures and languages are distributed as they are, why institutions exist, how resources and geography matter, and why present conditions exist.

It does not predetermine what the world becomes next.
