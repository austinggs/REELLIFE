Border rules
------------
Borders may follow rivers, mountains, coastlines, historical boundaries or negotiated lines. Countries may contain enclaves, exclaves, autonomous areas and disputed territories.

Country borders may change through:
- treaties
- wars
- independence
- negotiated territorial transfer
- annexation
- dissolution
- decolonization

Historical country state tracks former borders, governments, capitals, conflicts and population conditions.

International relationships
---------------------------
Countries can have diplomatic, trade, military, cultural, migration and historical relationships. Those relationships use the broader systems architecture rather than hard-coded one-off effects.

Country reputation uses the System 22 reputation model.

Exact country populations, currencies, ministries, budgets, named institutions and detailed relations remain current-state content rather than immutable country definition.
