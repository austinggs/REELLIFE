WORLD BUILD 12 — CURRENT WORLD STATE
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Canonical game-start date
--------------------------
1 January 2042

Global starting population
--------------------------
Approximately 6.8 billion

Global urbanization
-------------------
Approximately 70% of the population is urban or metropolitan at game start, with substantial variation by country.

Global demographic character
----------------------------
Aurelia contains:
- rapid-growth populations
- relatively stable populations
- aging populations

These are aggregate demographic patterns, not deterministic descriptions of individuals.

Economic state
--------------
Aurelia has a globally interconnected mixed economy containing private firms, state-linked enterprises, cooperatives, family businesses, nonprofits, public institutions, informal businesses and independent workers.

Technology state
----------------
Aurelia possesses advanced computing, smartphones, internet, cloud infrastructure, AI, robotics, satellites, advanced medicine, modern transportation, sophisticated manufacturing, renewable energy, nuclear energy and biotechnology, with uneven access.

Energy state
------------
Energy remains mixed between fossil fuels, nuclear and renewable systems. The global energy transition is underway but incomplete.

Labor market
------------
Labor markets are interconnected through local, national, remote and international work. Skill shortages, wage differences and technological change influence mobility.

Education
---------
Global literacy is high by historical standards. Education systems include public basic education, vocational education, universities, private institutions, apprenticeships and professional certification, with uneven access and quality.

Healthcare
----------
Medical capability ranges from advanced specialist systems in major metropolitan areas to more limited access in remote regions.

Migration
---------
Migration remains a major demographic force. Historical ties, language, economic opportunity, education, family networks and geography influence movement.

Religious landscape
-------------------
All seven major transregional traditions remain active alongside local traditions, denominations, syncretic communities, secular and nonreligious populations.

Language landscape
------------------
Major language families remain active across country borders. Multilingualism is common in ports, trade corridors, border regions and major cities.

Institutional state
-------------------
Aurelia contains governments, courts, schools, hospitals, universities, banks, corporations, unions, religious institutions, media organizations, NGOs/civil society and international bodies.

Institutional capacity varies by funding, staffing, trust, procedures, infrastructure and expertise.

Legal state
-----------
Countries maintain different legal systems covering identity, property, contracts, employment, family, criminal law, immigration, taxation and organizational activity.

International system
--------------------
International organizations address diplomacy, trade, finance, health, migration, environment, humanitarian issues, transportation and communications. Their effectiveness depends on member states, treaties, funding and institutional capacity.

Environmental state
-------------------
Aurelia faces climate change, water stress, deforestation, pollution, biodiversity loss, coastal pressure and extreme weather, with substantial regional variation.

Security state
--------------
The world is not in predetermined global war. It does contain territorial disputes, maritime claims, trade disputes, sanctions, military tensions, organized crime, cybercrime, political instability and historical grievances.

Information environment
-----------------------
Information is abundant and decentralized. Sources include television, newspapers, websites, social networks, messaging systems, organizations and personal networks.

Global economic interdependence
-------------------------------
Economic and geopolitical disruptions can propagate through trade, supply chains, prices, businesses, employment, household incomes and consumption.

World-start rule
----------------
The world is already active before the player begins. Governments, families, businesses, students, workers, criminals, researchers and institutions are already making decisions and experiencing events.

World Definition vs World State
--------------------------------
World Definition answers: What exists?
World State answers: What is happening right now?

The same world definition can support standard, historical, alternate, sandbox, scenario and custom modes.
