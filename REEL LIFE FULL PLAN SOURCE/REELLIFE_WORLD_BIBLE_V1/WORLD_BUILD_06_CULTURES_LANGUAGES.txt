WORLD BUILD 06 — CULTURES & LANGUAGES
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Core cultural principle
-----------------------
Culture is learned, transmitted, adapted and contested. Culture influences behavior but does not determine it.

Important distinctions
----------------------
- culture
- ethnicity
- nationality
- language
- religion
- citizenship
- personal identity

Cultural regions
----------------
Cultural regions may be:
- local
- regional
- cross-border
- historical
- diaspora-based
- urban
- occupational
- digital

Language families
-----------------
The approved first-pass build explicitly established six major language families. An earlier draft mistakenly referred to eight; no unsupported families are invented here.

1. Ardanic Family
   Languages: Ardan, Valed, Selvar, Torren, Caldic, Narethic

2. Veyric Family
   Languages: Veyric, Norvan, Edrian, Sorellan, Lethenic, Coravelan

3. Sahric Family
   Languages: Sahric, Namari, Keshic, Arashi, Tereni, Belaran, Omeran, Jandori

4. Orinthian Family
   Languages: Orinthian, Westmarker, Avelic, Tarsenic, Belmondian, Sarethic, Southern Coastal, Mariner's Creole

5. Kharic Family
   Languages: Kharic, Estaran, Varonic, Dorenic, Kalenic, Northern Kharic, Steppe Kharic

6. Ilyran Family
   Languages: Ilyran, Selini, Asteran, Eastern Ilyran, Lyrenic, Island Languages

Cultural expression
-------------------
Culture can shape:
- food
- clothing
- music
- art
- stories
- festivals
- etiquette
- ceremonies
- architecture
- family practices
- social expectations

Transmission
------------
Culture moves through:
- family
- peers
- schools
- religion
- workplaces
- neighborhoods
- media
- books
- entertainment
- institutions
- online communities

Identity
--------
Individual cultural identity is composite and dynamic. People can be multilingual, bicultural, diaspora-connected, highly traditional, hybridized, secular, religious or culturally detached from their ancestry.

Diffusion and change
--------------------
Cultures can:
- diffuse
- mix
- conflict
- revive
- adapt
- fragment
- hybridize
- change between generations
