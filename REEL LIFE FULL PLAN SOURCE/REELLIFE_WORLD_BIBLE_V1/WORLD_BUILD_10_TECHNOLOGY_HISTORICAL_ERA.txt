WORLD BUILD 10 — TECHNOLOGY & HISTORICAL ERA
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Current historical era
-----------------------
Contemporary Age

Technological baseline
----------------------
Aurelia at game start is technologically comparable to a highly developed early-to-mid 21st-century civilization, while remaining fictional and not a copy of Earth.

The world contains:
- advanced computing
- smartphones
- high-speed telecommunications
- internet
- cloud infrastructure
- advanced medicine
- commercial aviation
- modern automobiles
- satellites
- sophisticated manufacturing
- renewable energy
- nuclear power
- robotics
- artificial intelligence
- biotechnology

Technology is unevenly distributed across:
- countries
- cities
- organizations
- households
- individuals

Critical distinction
--------------------
Technology existence != availability != affordability != adoption.

A technology can exist globally but be inaccessible because of:
- cost
- infrastructure
- law
- skills
- supply constraints
- organizational capacity
- geography

Technology prerequisites and diffusion
---------------------------------------
Technologies depend on prerequisite knowledge, infrastructure, manufacturing capability, capital and skilled workers.

Diffusion occurs through:
- trade
- migration
- education
- espionage
- licensing
- foreign investment
- universities
- international cooperation
- imitation
- government programs

Technology and society
----------------------
Technology influences:
- employment
- education
- economy
- culture
- government
- warfare
- regulation
- ownership
- environment

Failure and obsolescence
------------------------
Technologies can fail, become incompatible, become obsolete, remain as legacy systems or reappear through niche use.

Innovation
----------
Research, experimentation, breakthroughs and adoption can produce economic and social cascades.
