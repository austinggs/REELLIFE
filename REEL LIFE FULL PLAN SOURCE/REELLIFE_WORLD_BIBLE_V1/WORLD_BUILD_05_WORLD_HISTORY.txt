WORLD BUILD 05 — WORLD HISTORY
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Historical eras
---------------
1. Deep Settlement
2. First Civilizations
3. Age of Kingdoms
4. Age of Networks
5. Age of States
6. Industrial Transformation
7. Modern Transformation
8. Contemporary Age

Historical causality
--------------------
Aurelia's history should be understood as a multi-level causal process:
Geography -> Settlement -> Population growth -> Technology -> Institutions -> Trade -> Conflict / Cooperation -> Political transformation -> Cultural change -> Modern world

Deep Settlement
---------------
Human settlement develops through multiple migration corridors rather than a single migration narrative. Population expands according to geography, climate, food availability, technology and social organization.

First Civilizations
-------------------
Agriculture emerges regionally based on climate, soils, water, suitable plants and animals, population pressure and accumulated knowledge.
Permanent villages become towns and cities. Early states develop from city alliances, hereditary leadership, religious authority, military organization, councils and confederations.

Major river civilizations form around the Ardan, Saren, Veyr and Orin systems.

Age of Kingdoms
---------------
Kingdoms and empires emerge across diverse regions. Political structures differ by geography and historical circumstance. Long-distance trade expands across deserts, mountains, steppe, coasts and inland seas.

Age of Networks
---------------
Maritime expansion grows through shipbuilding, navigation, astronomy and cartography. Trade, migration, religious exchange and cultural diffusion connect distant populations.

Empires integrate territories and later fragment. Migration periods occur because of climate, war, agriculture, political collapse, religion, famine, environmental pressure and opportunity.

Age of States
-------------
Bureaucratic state systems expand. Taxation, legal systems, identity records, passports, centralized administration and standing institutions become increasingly important.

Religion influences institutions, law, education, art, architecture, family organization and political legitimacy without mechanically determining behavior.

Cities become centers of markets, courts, universities, religious institutions and merchant organizations.

Modern intellectual developments include advances in mathematics, medicine, astronomy, engineering, agriculture, navigation, philosophy and political theory.

Printing and mass communication
-------------------------------
Printing accelerates literacy, education, political communication, religious debate, scientific exchange and public discourse.

Industrial Transformation
-------------------------
Steam power, manufacturing, railways, metallurgy, industrial agriculture and telegraphy transform production and transport. Cities expand rapidly and labor systems change.

Electricity and mass communications expand industrial capacity and social connectivity.

Global conflicts
----------------
Aurelia experiences major wars with distinct causes and consequences. Their historical effects include civilian losses, displacement, reconstruction, international-law developments and institutional change. Exact named wars remain future detailed content rather than a fully hand-authored chronology in this first-pass Bible.

Modern Transformation
---------------------
Decolonization reshapes political geography. International institutions expand. Computing, telecommunications, internet systems, mobile technology, cloud computing, automation and AI transform economic and social structures.

Globalization produces complex supply chains, international capital flows, migration networks and interdependence.

Historical inequality
---------------------
Unequal outcomes emerge from combinations of geography, colonial history, institutions, wars, industrialization, resources, education, political stability, trade and demography.

Historical memory
-----------------
Historical memory is preserved through:
- monuments
- archives
- school curricula
- family stories
- political narratives
- holidays
- museums
- place names
- historical grievances

Historical truth and narrative are distinct. Characters can know family stories that differ from archival evidence. The simulation knows the underlying historical state even when individuals do not.

History at multiple levels
--------------------------
- deep world history
- continental history
- regional history
- national history
- local history
- family history
- personal history

Resolution
----------
The World Bible does not attempt to hand-author every event across ten thousand years. Historical detail is multi-resolution. Broad eras and causal developments are canonical; exact named events can be added later using the same event architecture as the live simulation.
