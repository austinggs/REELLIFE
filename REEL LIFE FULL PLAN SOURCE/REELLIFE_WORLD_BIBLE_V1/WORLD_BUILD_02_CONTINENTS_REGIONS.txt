Regional structure
------------------
A Region is a geographic and ecological unit. A region is not automatically a country, culture, language group or political jurisdiction.

Countries may cross regional boundaries. Cultural regions may cross countries. Economic regions may also span multiple jurisdictions.

Regional design rules
---------------------
Regions should provide meaningful causal context for:
- settlement density
- agricultural productivity
- migration
- transportation
- economic specialization
- cultural exchange
- political boundaries
- resource extraction
- environmental risk

Regions must remain spatially coherent even as borders, settlements or infrastructure change over time.
