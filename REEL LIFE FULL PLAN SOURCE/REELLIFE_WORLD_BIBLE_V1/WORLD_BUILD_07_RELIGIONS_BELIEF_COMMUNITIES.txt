AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1

Core separation
---------------
The simulation distinguishes:
- belief
- religious identity
- religious practice
- religious community
- religious institution
- religious tradition
- spirituality
- nonbelief

Seven major transregional traditions
------------------------------------
1. The Covenant Faith
   Strong historical presence in western and central Elandra.
   Themes: covenant, moral law, responsibility, community, charity, justice, scholarship.

2. The Way of the Living Word
   Strong historical presence in the Veyran sphere.
   Themes: sacred teaching, compassion, personal transformation, community and service.
   Historical institutions include monasteries, schools and hospitals.

3. The Radiant Path
   Strong presence along Sahraen trade corridors.
   Themes: balance, discipline, enlightenment, compassion, self-knowledge and cycles of existence.

4. The Many-Hearth Traditions
   Family of local and regional traditions, especially in Sahraen and Orinth.
   Themes: ancestors, household spirits, sacred places, nature, community, seasons and oral tradition.

5. The River Teachings
   Historical roots in Ardan, Saren, Veyr and Orin river civilizations.
   Themes: harmony, responsibility, wisdom, learning, proper conduct and social/natural balance.
   These teachings may function as religious, philosophical or blended traditions.

6. The Sky and Earth Tradition
   Strong historical presence among northern and highland peoples.
   Themes: sky, land, ancestors, seasons, animals and stewardship.

7. The Open Hand Philosophy
   Ethical and philosophical movement centered on human dignity, compassion, mutual aid, reason and social equality.
   It can function as secular humanism or a broader ethical philosophy.

Other religious and nonreligious life
-------------------------------------
Aurelia contains:
- local religions
- denominations
- syncretic traditions
- minority traditions
- diaspora religious communities
- personal spiritual practices
- secular worldviews
- nonreligious and atheist populations

Religious change
----------------
Religion can change through:
- birth
- death
- migration
- conversion
- deconversion
- intermarriage
- education
- institutional influence
- personal experience
- family transmission

Religion and social systems
---------------------------
Religion may interact with:
- family
- law
- education
- community organization
- architecture
- holidays
- charity
- political legitimacy
- migration
- identity

Religion does not automatically determine political or individual behavior.
