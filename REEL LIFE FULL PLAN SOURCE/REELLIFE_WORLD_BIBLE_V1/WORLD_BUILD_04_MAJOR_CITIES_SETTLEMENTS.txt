WORLD BUILD 04 — MAJOR CITIES & SETTLEMENTS
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Settlement hierarchy
--------------------
Continent
-> Country
-> Region
-> Settlement System
-> Megacity / Major Metropolitan City / Large City / Medium City / Small City / Town / Village / Rural Community / Specialized Settlement / Temporary or Seasonal Settlement

Cities and settlements emerge from combinations of:
- freshwater
- fertile land
- rivers
- ports
- trade
- minerals
- transport
- defense
- administration
- religion
- education
- industry
- employment
- migration
- historical inertia

Metropolitan structure
----------------------
A metropolitan area may contain:
- core city
- suburbs
- satellite cities
- industrial zones
- airport districts
- agricultural fringes
- independent municipalities

Population and growth
---------------------
Exact city populations are intentionally not fixed at this stage. They will be reconciled from:
- geography
- history
- fertility/mortality
- migration
- employment
- housing
- infrastructure
- urbanization
- economic opportunity

Cities can rise, decline, densify, suburbanize, stagnate, recover, fragment or become partially abandoned. Historical settlements may remain as archaeological or cultural locations even after depopulation.

District and neighborhood layers
--------------------------------
Cities contain districts and neighborhoods whose:
- population
- housing
- land use
- infrastructure
- organizations
- commercial activity
- social composition
- transportation
change dynamically.

Population simulation
---------------------
Settlement populations use the aggregate-to-materialized-individual model from System 07. The city exists as a statistical population at low resolution and can generate relevant households and individuals when required by the simulation.
