WORLD BUILD 09 — INSTITUTIONS & ORGANIZATIONS
AURELIA WORLD BIBLE V1
============================================================

Institution vs organization
---------------------------
Institution = durable social system or framework.
Organization = concrete entity operating within one or more institutions.

Universal organization model
----------------------------

- owners

- departments

Institutional memory
--------------------
Institutions and organizations retain knowledge through:
- records
- procedures
- precedents
- policies
- reputation
- employee knowledge
- institutional history

Organizations can gain, lose or distort institutional memory.

Organizational dynamics
-----------------------
Organizations can experience:
- founding
- growth
- restructuring
- hiring
- leadership change
- mergers
- acquisitions
- branch creation
- branch closure
- failure
- bankruptcy
- reputational damage
- recovery
- organizational politics
- path dependence

Institutional capacity
----------------------
Institution quality is not a single score. Relevant dimensions include:
- funding
- staffing
- administrative capacity
- public trust
- service reach
- expertise
- bottlenecks
- procedures
- infrastructure

Organizations affect cities and communities through employment, investment, infrastructure, services and culture.
