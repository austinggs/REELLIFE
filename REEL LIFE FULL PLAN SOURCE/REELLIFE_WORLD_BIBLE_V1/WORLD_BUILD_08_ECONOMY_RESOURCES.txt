------------------
Aurelia's economy is a network of people, households, organizations, resources, infrastructure, knowledge, contracts, institutions and decisions.

- state-owned enterprises
- nonprofits

- multinational corporations
- independent workers

Major economic zones
--------------------
Ardan Basin / Elandra:
Agriculture, river transport, manufacturing, finance, logistics, technology and construction.

Veyran Heartland / Central Basin / Westhaven:
Agriculture, manufacturing, finance, technology and maritime trade.

Sahraen:
Desert trade, river agriculture, mining, pastoralism, coastal fishing, manufacturing, renewable energy and logistics.

Orinth:
Agriculture, livestock, mining, manufacturing and maritime trade.

Kharos:
Minerals, forestry, livestock, southern agriculture, energy and overland trade.

Ilyra:
Shipping, fishing, shipbuilding, tourism, finance, trade, maritime services and specialized manufacturing.
